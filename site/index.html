Yours

Your folder. Your key. No server of ours in between.

Your courses are files on your disk, your key is sealed in the system keychain where there is one, and there are no analytics. Three things are worth knowing.

1

Build a course from a folder.

Point it at your slides and past papers. A second pass, the Checker, re-solves every auto-marked answer against your files, throws out what they contradict, and leaves its report in the course.

2

Your own key, with a ceiling.

Paste a DeepSeek or OpenRouter key. It stops at $1 a course and $2 a day unless you change that, and Settings shows what each thing cost. Exact answers are marked by code, not by the model.

3

What leaves your computer.

Only what you use, and only to where it has to go.

Run it from source

Node 22 or newer, and pnpm 10.

git clone https://github.com/bilimind/desktop.git
cd desktop
corepack enable
pnpm install
pnpm start
