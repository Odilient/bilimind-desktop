# dormathon-briefing: local only, not redistributed

`dormathon-briefing.pdf` is the organisers' Dormathon 2026 briefing deck ("AI SLIDES", made in Canva, 41 slides),
copied from the team laptop (`C:\Users\User\Desktop\AI SLIDES .pdf`). It is organiser material: all rights stay with
the Dormathon 2026 organisers and the sponsors shown on the slides. It also lists committee members' phone numbers.

- It is **git-ignored** (`showcase/sources/dormathon-briefing/*.pdf` in the repo `.gitignore`) and must never be
  committed, published or shipped inside a course folder that leaves this laptop.
- Use: an optional, fun live input on stage ("turn the organisers' own briefing into a course").
- The copy here was re-compressed (images downsampled to about 96 dpi, 68 MB to 11.7 MB). The text layer is
  identical to the original, page by page (checked with PyMuPDF).
- 7 of the 41 pages (6, 9-14: sponsor slides) are images with no text layer; the ingest should report them as
  scanned pages.
