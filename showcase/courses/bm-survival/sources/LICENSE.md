# bm-survival: sources, licence and attribution

Cameo showcase input for "Bahasa Melayu Survival Kit".

| File | What it is | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| `malay-survival-phrasebook.md` | Phrasebook for an international student's first month: pronunciation, greetings, polite forms, numbers and money, mamak ordering, Grab, campus, time, problems | CC BY-SA 4.0 (adapted from Wikivoyage) |
| `bmk2-note.md` | One-paragraph note: Taylor's lists Bahasa Melayu Komunikasi 2 (MPU31002) for international degree students | Our own text; facts from the cited Taylor's page |

## Attribution

`malay-survival-phrasebook.md` is adapted from:

- Wikivoyage contributors, "Malay phrasebook", Wikivoyage, https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Malay_phrasebook
  (accessed 10 October 2026), licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

Changes: the article's phrases were selected, reorganised into tables and rewritten; some translations were
adjusted to everyday Malaysian usage (for example *esok* for "tomorrow", *Selamat petang* as "good afternoon /
early evening"), each with a note. Rows marked † (mamak ordering, Grab, campus, polite forms and particles) were
written for this demo. The adapted file is shared under the same licence, CC BY-SA 4.0.

`bmk2-note.md` cites: Taylor's University, "Malaysian Compulsory and General Studies Modules",
https://university.taylors.edu.my/en/student-life/manage-your-studies/malaysian-compulsory-and-general-studies-modules.html
(accessed 10 October 2026). No text was copied from that page.

## Honest labels

- "A practice companion, not the official BMK syllabus." appears at the top of the phrasebook and in the note.
- The phrases were checked by the demo team against Wikivoyage and standard Malaysian usage; a native Malaysian
  speaker should check them before the demo (plan item H15).
