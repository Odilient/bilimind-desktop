# Note: Bahasa Melayu Komunikasi 2 at Taylor's University

International students on Taylor's University degree programmes take **Bahasa Melayu Komunikasi 2**
(module code **MPU31002**, 2 credits) as one of their MPU modules. MPU modules (Mata Pelajaran Pengajian Umum)
are the compulsory general studies modules of Malaysian higher education. Taylor's groups them into four
categories, U1 to U4, and its page says students must take all the MPU modules listed under each category.
For international degree students, Bahasa Melayu Komunikasi 2 sits in category U1, next to Kursus Integriti dan
Antirasuah and Citra Malaysia. Malaysian students take different U1 modules instead of Bahasa Melayu Komunikasi 2.

The survival phrasebook in this folder is a practice companion for a student's first month (mamak, Grab, campus)
and a gentle start on Bahasa Melayu Komunikasi. It is not the official BMK syllabus and does not replace the module.

Source: Taylor's University, "Malaysian Compulsory and General Studies Modules",
https://university.taylors.edu.my/en/student-life/manage-your-studies/malaysian-compulsory-and-general-studies-modules.html
(accessed 10 October 2026). Module names, code, credits and categories are as listed on that page on that date; check
the page for the current list.
