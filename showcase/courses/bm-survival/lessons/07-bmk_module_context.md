# What BMK 2 at Taylor's actually is

> [!info] Stuff to memorise · 10 min · study Sat 17 Oct
> You will be able to say what Bahasa Melayu Komunikasi 2 is and where it sits in your degree. Covers module code MPU31002, its 2 credits, the MPU general-studies requirement, and Taylor's U1 to U4 categories. Prevents treating your phrasebook as the syllabus, when the module is assessed and this companion is only practice.

## A common mix-up

> [!failure] The belief
> My phrasebook is my BMK 2 syllabus — if I learn the phrases, I've covered the module.

**Why it seems right.** The phrasebook is where the Malay actually is, and you can hold it in your hand. BMK 2 is a class you sit in, so the two feel like the same thing at different levels of detail.

> [!success] What is true
> BMK 2 is an assessed module with its own code, credit value and category; the phrasebook is unassessed practice that supports it. The difference predicts which one shows up on your transcript: the phrasebook never does, and skipping the module's own requirements costs you credits regardless of how well you order at a mamak.

## Explainer

BMK 2 is not a phrasebook. It is a module on your degree record, and it has a code, a credit value and a category, the same way your programme's other modules do.

**Module code: MPU31002.** That is the code to look for when you register and on your timetable. Learn it as a string, not as a description.

**2 credits.** It carries two credits toward your degree. Two credits sounds small, which is exactly why it is easy to treat as optional. It is not optional: MPU modules are compulsory, and Taylor's says students must take all the MPU modules listed under each category.

**It sits in the MPU group.** MPU (*Mata Pelajaran Pengajian Umum*) modules are the compulsory general-studies modules of Malaysian higher education, taken alongside your programme. They are not a Taylor's invention and not a language-school add-on: they are a national requirement that Taylor's delivers.

**Taylor's groups MPU modules into categories U1 to U4.** For international degree students, BMK 2 sits in U1, next to *Kursus Integriti dan Antirasuah* and *Citra Malaysia*. Malaysian students take different U1 modules instead of BMK 2, so your BMK class is made up of international students like you.

The practical consequence: BMK 2 is assessed. Your phrasebook is not. Use the phrasebook to survive the mamak, the Grab and the campus — and use it to arrive at BMK 2 already able to say something. Then let the module itself tell you what it assesses.

## Key points

- Bahasa Melayu Komunikasi 2 is a module on your degree record, not a phrasebook or a club.
- Its module code is MPU31002.
- It carries 2 credits, so it counts toward the degree even though the number is small.
- It is an MPU module: MPU modules are the compulsory general-studies modules of Malaysian higher education.
- For international degree students at Taylor's it sits in category U1; Malaysian students take different U1 modules instead.
- The module is assessed; your phrasebook is unassessed practice that supports it.
- Learning phrases helps you in class, but it does not stand in for completing the module.
