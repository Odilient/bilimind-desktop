## Cut, and why

- **When to use Assalamualaikum**: A side lesson: the greetings lesson already teaches Assalamualaikum and its reply; the 2 weeks go to mamak, Grab and campus, which you asked for.
- **Sentences you can build yourself**: Not needed to survive the first month: every lesson teaches whole phrases you can use as they are.
- **Little words you will hear everywhere**: A side lesson on lah, jom and kan: nice to have, but 20 minutes a day do not fit it next to mamak, Grab and campus.
- **When it goes wrong: lost, sick, help**: A side lesson; the 2 weeks go to mamak, Grab and campus. Remember 999 (112 from a mobile) and Tolong! (Help!).
