## Cut, and why

- **Booking your MOH food handler course**: Folded into Who is a food handler: the certificate is one line of SOP section 2 and that lesson teaches it.
- **Answering "is this dairy-free?"**: Scenario A (the dairy question) is taught inside the allergens lesson; a second lesson on it would repeat it.
- **Cooking pearls and brewing tea to spec**: Pearl and tea prep is cut to fit the week; the opening-checklist lesson covers when pearls and tea start.
- **When a batch of pearls goes wrong**: Builds on the cut pearl-prep lesson.
- **When a customer says it is too sweet**: A two-line SOP scenario (remake once, free of charge); the drink-building lesson covers the sweetness pumps.
- **Capstone: open the kiosk and serve ten drinks**: A 90-minute supervised shift at the kiosk; it happens on the bar, not in a browser course.
