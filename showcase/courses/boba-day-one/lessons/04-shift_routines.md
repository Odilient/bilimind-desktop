# Opening and closing the kiosk by the checklist

> [!info] A skill you do · 30 min · study Tue 13 Oct
> You will be able to run the 09:30 to 10:00 opening checklist and the 21:30 to 22:30 closing checklist without missing a step. Covers the shift table — opening 10:00 to 16:00 with a 09:30 arrival — the opening steps, and the closing cut-offs: stop cooking pearls at 21:30, last orders 21:55, discard all cooked pearls. Prevents a bar opened without handwashing and pearls carried into the next day.

## Explainer

A kiosk that opens late loses the 10:00 rush. A kiosk that closes sloppy carries cooked pearls into tomorrow, and the SOP is clear that cooked pearls are made fresh every day and thrown away at close.

Your SOP splits the day into two checklists. Section 3 of the SOP sets the shifts:

| Shift | Hours | Arrive by | Runs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opening | 10:00 to 16:00 | 09:30 | Opening checklist (section 4) |
| Closing | 16:00 to 22:30 | 15:50 | Closing checklist (section 5) |

The opening shift arrives at 09:30 with thirty minutes before the doors open. That half hour is not slack: it holds the handwash, the uniform, the chiller and freezer readings, the date labels, the first batch of pearls (about 50 minutes), the tea, the ice, sanitising and the sealer test. Skip one and you either open late or open with equipment that has not been verified.

The closing shift arrives at 15:50 and finishes at 22:30. The shop closes at 22:00, but the closing checklist runs from 21:30 to 22:30 because the last hour is where the cut-offs live: stop cooking new pearls at 21:30, last orders at 21:55, and every cooked pearl in the pot goes in the bin before you lock up. The SOP is explicit that cooked pearls, brewed tea and opened fresh milk and oat milk are prepared fresh every day — they are discarded, not stored.

Two failures the checklists exist to prevent: a bar opened without anyone washing their hands, and pearls carried into the next day. Both are invisible at the time.

## Step by step

The opening checklist is section 4 of the SOP, run from 09:30 so the kiosk opens at 10:00. Each step below is one bullet of section 4, in the SOP's order, with the [LAW] or [SHOP] tag the SOP prints beside it.

### 1. Wash your hands with soap for at least 20 seconds

Do this before touching anything behind the bar. [LAW, reg. 33(1)(c)]

> [!warning]
> Starting the pearl pot first because it takes longest means everything you touched on the way in is now on the pot handle.

> [!check] Check
> Hands washed with soap for a counted 20 seconds before anything behind the bar is touched.

### 2. Put on the uniform and take off jewellery

Shop T-shirt, light-coloured apron, cap, closed shoes. Remove rings, watches, bracelets and pins. [LAW, regs. 32 and 33(1)(f)]

> [!warning]
> A ring or watch behind the bar breaks reg. 33(1)(f), whatever the shift.

> [!check] Check
> Apron, cap and closed shoes on; nothing on your fingers or wrists.

### 3. Read the chiller and freezer thermometers and log both

Write both readings in the temperature log. The chiller must be 4 °C or below, the freezer -18 °C or below. If the chiller is above 5 °C, tell the shift lead before using any milk. [SHOP]

> [!warning]
> Writing a number you did not read makes the log useless: the 5 °C rule only works if the reading is real.

> [!check] Check
> Two readings in the log; a chiller above 5 °C has gone to the shift lead before any milk is used.

### 4. Check date labels

Throw away anything past its use-by time and record it in the waste log. Use stock first in, first out. [LAW, reg. 38(1)(i)]

> [!warning]
> An expired item left on the shelf goes into the first drink that needs it.

> [!check] Check
> Nothing past its use-by time; every discard in the waste log.

### 5. Start the first batch of pearls

Follow section 6.1. A batch takes about 50 minutes, so start it first, before the tea.

> [!warning]
> A late pot means no ready pearls at 10:00, and the 25-minute rest in step 3 of section 6.1 cannot be skipped.

> [!check] Check
> The pearl water is at a rolling boil or the pearls are cooking.

### 6. Brew the teas and check the syrup

Brew black tea and jasmine green tea (section 6.2). Check the brown sugar syrup; make more if the bottle is under half full (section 6.3).

> [!warning]
> Steeping too long makes the tea bitter: black tea 8 minutes, jasmine green tea 6 minutes.

> [!check] Check
> Both teas strained into the urn and labelled with the time; syrup bottle at least half full.

### 7. Fill the ice bin with the ice scoop

Never scoop ice with a cup or your hands. Leave the scoop in its holder, handle up. [SHOP; ice must be protected from contamination, LAW reg. 35(1)(j)]

> [!warning]
> A cup used as a scoop can chip into the ice, and hands contaminate it.

> [!check] Check
> Ice bin full; scoop in its holder, handle up.

### 8. Sanitise the counter and equipment

Sanitise the counter, shakers, jugs and the sealing machine plate.

> [!warning]
> Last night's wash does not cover what touched the counter since.

> [!check] Check
> Counter, shakers, jugs and sealing plate sanitised.

### 9. Load the cup film and test the sealer

Load the cup film and test the sealing machine on an empty cup.

> [!warning]
> An untested sealer is found out on the first customer's drink.

> [!check] Check
> An empty test cup sealed flat and tight.

### 10. Set up the counter and open at 10:00

Turn on the POS, check the RM 200 float in the cash drawer, put out the DuitNow QR stand, the menu and the allergen board. Open at 10:00.

> [!warning]
> Opening without the allergen board leaves the cashier without the one thing section 9 says to check with the customer.

> [!check] Check
> POS on, RM 200 float counted, QR stand, menu and allergen board out, doors open at 10:00.

## Checklist

**Closing checklist, 21:30 to 22:30**

- [ ] 21:30 — stop cooking new pearls
  - *Anything started after 21:30 cannot be sold before close and must be discarded.*
- [ ] 21:55 — last orders taken
  - *Orders after 21:55 cannot be made and cleaned up before 22:30.*
- [ ] All cooked pearls, brewed tea, opened fresh milk and oat milk thrown away and recorded in the waste log
  - *These are prepared fresh every day; carrying them overnight is the failure this checklist exists to prevent.*
- [ ] Shakers, jugs, scoops and the pearl pot washed with detergent, rinsed, sanitised, air-dried upside down
  - *Detergent residue left on a shaker taints the first drink of the next shift.*
- [ ] Sealing machine unplugged; sealing plate wiped only once cool
  - *Wiping a hot plate burns you and warps the plate.*
- [ ] Ice bin emptied, wiped dry, lid left open
  - *A closed, damp ice bin grows mould overnight.*
- [ ] All food put away or covered BEFORE sweeping and mopping
  - *Sweeping near open food is a food-safety breach.*
- [ ] Counters, POS screen and menu boards wiped; floor swept and mopped
  - *Sticky counters and screens are the first thing the morning shift touches.*
- [ ] Rubbish taken to the bin centre and new bin liners put in
  - *Rubbish left overnight in a warm kiosk attracts pests.*
- [ ] Chiller and freezer temperatures written in the log
  - *A gap in the log is treated as an unmonitored unit.*
- [ ] Cash drawer counted with the shift lead and POS closed
  - *A drawer counted alone cannot be reconciled against a dispute.*
- [ ] Lights, water heater and tea urn switched off, shop locked, photo of the completed checklist posted in the crew group chat
  - *The photo is the only proof the checklist was actually run.*

> [!note]
> The SOP states cooked pearls, brewed tea and opened fresh milk and oat milk are prepared fresh every day and are discarded at close. There is no 'keep it for tomorrow' option. If you are unsure whether something was opened today, throw it away and log it.

## A common mix-up

> [!failure] The belief
> If I stop cooking pearls at 21:30 but there are still pearls in the pot at 22:00, I can leave them for the opening shift to use first thing tomorrow.

**Why it seems right.** The pearls look and smell fine, the pot is covered, and throwing away food that was cooked ninety minutes ago feels wasteful. On a busy night there can be a full pot left, and binning it feels like admitting you cooked too many.

> [!success] What is true
> The SOP states that cooked pearls, brewed tea and opened fresh milk and oat milk are prepared fresh every day and are discarded at close. There is no keep-for-tomorrow option, however good they look; section 6.1 already limits cooked pearls to 4 hours in the warmer. The waste log exists so that over-cooking shows up as a number the manager can act on, instead of as a pot someone quietly keeps.

## Project

**Run one opening and one closing under observation**

You have read both checklists. Now run them.

**Scenario.** You are on the opening shift on a Tuesday and the closing shift on the same day. A shift lead watches both and signs off.

**Constraints.**
- Opening: arrive by 09:30, doors open at 10:00. You may not skip a step to make the time — if you are behind, the shift lead decides what to cut, not you.
- Closing: the 21:30 pearl cut-off and the 21:55 last-orders cut-off are hard. You may not serve a drink after 21:55 and you may not start a pearl batch after 21:30.
- Every discard goes in the waste log with an amount, including anything you throw away because you were unsure whether it was opened today.
- The photo of the completed closing checklist goes in the crew group chat before you lock up (section 5).

**Done looks like.** Two completed checklists, both photographed and posted, with no step marked done that you did not actually do, and a waste log entry for every discard.

Deliverables:

- [ ] A completed opening checklist with both thermometer readings in the temperature log and the RM 200 float checked, signed off by the shift lead before 10:00.
- [ ] A completed closing checklist with the 21:30 and 21:55 cut-offs confirmed, photographed and posted before you leave.
- [ ] A waste log entry for every pearl, tea, milk and oat milk discard, with amounts.
- [ ] A 100-word note for the shift lead naming the one step you nearly missed and what made you nearly miss it.
