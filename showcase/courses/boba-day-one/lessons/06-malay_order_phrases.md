# Taking an order in Malay

> [!info] Said out loud · 20 min · study Thu 15 Oct
> You will be able to take an order spoken in Malay and write the correct sticker from it. Covers "Teh susu kurang manis, kurang ais, satu" as one milk tea, less sweet, less ice — CT01-R 50% LI — and the "bungkus" wording a takeaway order uses. Prevents writing 100% sweetness because you only caught the words "teh susu".

## Explainer

A customer steps up and says six words. You catch **teh susu** and reach for the sticker. If that is all you caught, you will write **CT01-R 100% RI** — and the customer will send it back, because they said **kurang manis** and **kurang ais**.

Section 7 of your SOP gives the mapping. The customer's words and the sticker code are two different languages, and your job is the translation:

| Customer says | Sticker |
|---|---|
| manis biasa / normal sugar | 100% |
| kurang manis sikit | 75% |
| kurang manis / half sugar | 50% |
| sikit manis / less sugar | 25% |
| tak nak gula / kosong / no sugar | 0% |

"Teh susu kurang manis, kurang ais, satu" is one milk tea, less sweet, less ice: **CT01-R 50% LI**. Three facts, three parts of the sticker. **Satu** is the quantity — one cup. It never changes the recipe.

Ice works the same way: **kurang ais** is LI, **tak nak ais** is NI, **ais biasa** is RI, **panas** is HOT. Section 7 also sets the floor on brown sugar drinks: BS01 and BS02 cannot go below 25% because the syrup is what makes the stripes. If a customer asks for 0% on a BS drink, offer 25% or a CT01 at 0% with pearls.

One more word decides where the cup goes. **Bungkus** — or **tapau** — means takeaway. **Minum sini** means drinking here. Same drink, same sticker, different bag.

## Three orders, three stickers

**Order 1.** "Teh susu kurang manis, kurang ais, satu."

- teh susu → CT01, regular
- kurang manis → 50%
- kurang ais → LI
- satu → one cup

**Sticker: CT01-R 50% LI**

---

**Order 2.** "BS01 besar, sikit manis, bungkus."

- BS01 → Brown Sugar Milk Tea
- besar → L (large)
- sikit manis → 25%
- bungkus → takeaway

**Sticker: BS01-L 25% RI** — ice is normal because nothing was said about it.

---

**Order 3.** "Teh susu, tak nak gula, kosong."

- teh susu → CT01, regular
- tak nak gula / kosong → 0%

**Sticker: CT01-R 0% RI.** This one is fine — CT01 is not a brown sugar drink, so 0% is allowed. The same request on a BS01 gets the section 7 offer instead: 25%, or a CT01 at 0% with pearls.

## Talk it through

A customer walks up to the counter and orders in Malay. You are the crew member on the register. You must understand the order, confirm it back, and write the correct sticker.

You are the crew member; the other person is the customer. Goal: Take the customer's order in Malay, confirm the sweetness, ice and takeaway choice back to them, and state the sticker you will write.
