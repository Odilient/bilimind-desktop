# Menu codes, prices and what each drink contains

> [!info] Stuff to memorise · 25 min · study Wed 14 Oct
> You will be able to read a code off a sticker and say the drink, the price and what it contains. Covers the menu codes — BS01 Brown Sugar Milk Tea with Pearls at RM 10.90 regular and RM 12.90 large, BS02, CT01 — the sweetness scale in 10 ml pumps, the ice levels, and which syrup each drink family uses. Prevents charging the regular price for a 700 ml cup and reaching for the wrong syrup.

## Explainer

A sticker reads `BS01-L 50% LI OAT +PD ALG:milk`. You have four seconds to turn that into a drink, a price and a warning. The price is not RM 10.90 — large is RM 12.90. The syrup is not fructose — BS01 uses brown sugar syrup. And `ALG:milk` plus `+PD` is a stop: egg pudding contains milk, so the cashier stops and explains before taking payment.

Section 8 of the SOP lists nine drinks. Learn the code, the two prices and the contains line as one unit:

| Code | Drink | Regular | Large | Contains |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| BS01 | Brown Sugar Milk Tea with Pearls | RM 10.90 | RM 12.90 | Milk; caffeine |
| BS02 | Brown Sugar Fresh Milk with Pearls (no tea) | RM 11.90 | RM 13.90 | Milk |
| CT01 | Classic Milk Tea | RM 7.90 | RM 9.90 | Milk (creamer has milk protein); soy; caffeine |
| CT02 | Jasmine Green Milk Tea | RM 8.90 | RM 10.90 | Milk (creamer has milk protein); soy; caffeine |
| TH01 | Thai Milk Tea | RM 8.90 | RM 10.90 | Milk (condensed and evaporated milk); caffeine |
| MT01 | Matcha Latte | RM 11.90 | RM 13.90 | Milk; caffeine |
| FT01 | Passion Fruit Green Tea | RM 8.90 | RM 10.90 | No milk in recipe; caffeine |
| FT02 | Lychee Oolong Tea | RM 9.90 | RM 11.90 | No milk in recipe; caffeine |
| FT03 | Lemon Black Tea | RM 7.90 | RM 9.90 | No milk in recipe; caffeine |

Two families split the syrup. Brown sugar drinks — BS01 and BS02 — use brown sugar syrup. Every other drink uses fructose syrup. The pump counts are identical either way, so the only thing that changes is which bottle you reach for. Section 7 sets one pump at 10 ml: regular 100% is 4 pumps, large 100% is 6 pumps. Large is not a fixed number of pumps more than regular: 100% is 4 and 6, 75% is 3 and 5, 50% is 2 and 3, 25% is 1 and 2, 0% is 0 and 0. Read the row.

Ice has its own sticker codes: NI, LI, RI, HOT. Regular normal ice is one 120 g scoop; large normal ice is 1.5 scoops, 170 g. HOT exists only for CT01, CT02 and MT01, regular size only.

## Reading `BS01-L 50% LI OAT +PD ALG:milk` end to end

Split the sticker into its six fields.

1. `BS01` — Brown Sugar Milk Tea with Pearls. Brown sugar family, so the syrup is brown sugar syrup, not fructose.
2. `-L` — large, 700 ml. Price is RM 12.90, not RM 10.90.
3. `50%` — 2 pumps regular, 3 pumps large. Large, so 3 pumps, 30 ml.
4. `LI` — less ice. Large, so 1 scoop, 85 g (half a scoop, 60 g, is the regular size).
5. `OAT` — swap to oat milk, +RM 2.00. This is a gluten flag: the SOP says the oat milk is not certified gluten-free.
6. `+PD` — egg pudding, +RM 2.00. Contains egg and milk.

Price if it were servable: RM 12.90 + RM 2.00 + RM 2.00 = RM 16.90.

But `ALG:milk` is on the sticker. The customer has a milk allergy. OAT replaces the fresh milk, but `+PD` contains milk, so the drink as written is not safe. Section 8 of the SOP is explicit: the cashier must stop and explain before taking payment. You do not make this drink. You explain, offer a drink with no milk in the recipe (FT01, FT02 or FT03) without cheese foam or pudding, follow the allergy steps in section 9, and let the customer choose.

## A common mix-up

> [!failure] The belief
> A large cup is just more of the same drink, so I charge the regular price and use the regular pump count.

**Why it seems right.** The code is the same — BS01 is BS01 — and the drink tastes the same, so the size feels like a detail rather than a different line item.

> [!success] What is true
> Section 8 lists two prices per code: BS01 is RM 10.90 regular and RM 12.90 large. Section 7 lists two pump counts per sweetness level: 100% is 4 pumps regular and 6 pumps large. Charging RM 10.90 for a 700 ml cup undercharges by RM 2.00, and using 4 pumps in a large cup makes the drink weak. The size letter on the sticker decides both the price and the pump count.

## Checklist

**Before you call a drink back to the bar**

- [ ] Code read and drink named — BS01, CT02, FT03 and so on
  - *Prevents pulling the wrong base tea or the wrong syrup bottle*
- [ ] Size letter read: regular or large, and the matching price said out loud
  - *Prevents charging RM 10.90 for a 700 ml cup*
- [ ] Sweetness percentage converted to pumps for that size
  - *Prevents 4 pumps going into a large cup*
- [ ] Ice code read: NI, LI, RI or HOT
  - *Prevents the wrong scoop weight and prevents HOT being taken for a large cup*
- [ ] Syrup family confirmed: brown sugar for BS01 and BS02, fructose for everything else
  - *Prevents reaching for the wrong squeeze bottle*
- [ ] Every add-on priced from the add-on table, not from memory
  - *Prevents a RM 2.00 add-on being charged as RM 1.50*
- [ ] Allergy flag checked against the contains column of every component
  - *Prevents serving +PD or +CF to a milk-allergic customer*
- [ ] If the customer said kurang manis with no number, set 50% and tell them
  - *Prevents a silent guess that the customer did not agree to*

> [!note]
> Tick these in order on the sticker itself. If any line cannot be ticked, stop and ask the shift lead before the drink goes to the bar.
